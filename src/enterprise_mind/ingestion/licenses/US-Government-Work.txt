17 U.S.C. 105 states:
Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the
United States Government, but the United States Government is not precluded
from receiving and holding copyrights transferred to it by assignment,
bequest, or otherwise.

GovInfo explains that U.S. Government work means work prepared by an officer
or employee as part of official duties. Government publications may contain
copyrighted material used with permission; publication does not authorize reuse
of that material without the owner's consent. Consult the originating agency
where clearance is uncertain. Give customary credit to the originating agency.
Licensed website images are not automatically public domain.
